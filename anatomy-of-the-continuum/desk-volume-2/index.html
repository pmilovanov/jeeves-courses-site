How to Use This Volume

This volume is the desk’s half of the second season, and the partnership’s terms are unchanged from Volume One: the episodes carry motivation, history, definitions in words, and the strategy of proofs; this volume carries precise notation, formal statements, complete proofs, and the problems. Listen first, then work. Nothing appears here that the corresponding episode has not motivated, and nothing is claimed aloud as proved unless the proof appears here — or the narration says plainly where the full argument lives, or that it lies beyond the course.

Standing conventions, carried over.  The natural numbers begin at one. Facts used on credit are flagged where they occur, entered in the running ledger, and paid in a named later chapter or declared honestly beyond the course. Each chapter opens with an unnumbered Episode Digest and a Notation Ledger; problems come in three tiers (Calisthenics; The Real Thing; For the Ambitious, starred); hints are spoiler-safe; solutions are complete and terse for Tier I, provided for selected Tier II problems, and withheld for Tier III. The bell of completeness $(\circledast)$ counts marquee deployments only, and its count continues from Volume One: fourteen rings stand on the register as this volume opens.

What this season assumes.  Volume One entire — in particular: suprema and infima and their approximation properties; countability and the diagonal; sequences, series, and the huddle; open, closed, compact, and Baire’s theorem; continuity and uniform continuity; the derivative; the Riemann integral, its fundamental theorem, and its discontents. Where a Volume One item is load-bearing, the chapters cite it by chapter and number.
