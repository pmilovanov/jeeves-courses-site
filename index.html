The Jeeves Courses
Audio courses of unusual thoroughness, narrated by Jeeves

A shelf of audio courses produced, one evening at a time, for a single exacting gentleman, and now offered to the general public in the belief that rigour and good manners are not rivals. Each course is a season of spoken-word episodes — scripts written for the ear, performed by whatever competent voice one has to hand — accompanied by a Desk Volume in which the formal statements, complete proofs, and tiered problems are set down properly. One listens in the armchair; one verifies at the desk.

The Parliament of Machines
An Audio Course in Distributed Systems
Season complete · twelve episodes · a thirteen-chapter Desk Volume

Twelve evenings on the science of unreliable computers in unreliable company: time and the ordering of events, what mathematics forbids, Paxos derived scar by scar rather than recited, replication and its schools, consistency as a field guide, transactions at a distance, partitioning, the log, and the engineering of failure — conducted throughout as the proceedings of a small and part-time parliament. The Desk Volume sets down the formal statements, complete proofs, protocol boxes, trace exhibits, and tiered problems that the ear can only sketch.

The Anatomy of the Continuum
An Audio Course in Real Analysis, in Two Seasons
Series complete · sixteen episodes · two Desk Volumes

The real line built from first principles and then, at last, measured properly. Season One anatomizes the continuum — what a number actually is, Cantor and the sizes of infinity, the topology of the line, continuity earned, the derivative and its monsters, the Riemann integral and its discontents. Season Two reopens the establishment under a new sign — The Measure of All Things — for Lebesgue measure, Vitali and Banach–Tarski, integration rebuilt, the convergence theorems, Fubini, and probability revealed as measure theory in evening dress. Spoken entirely without symbols; the two Desk Volumes supply them in full.

Reading the Mind of the Machine
A Course in Mechanistic Interpretability
Independence Edition · fourteen episodes · a fourteen-chapter Desk Volume with sixty-nine labs

Fourteen episodes reading the transformer closely. The course proper follows the single forward pass — the residual stream and the geometry of features, attention read closely, induction heads and the origin of in-context learning, superposition, the sparse autoencoder in full, circuits and the discipline of intervention, steering and the stakes. The supplement descends to the engine room and the biography: transcoders and crosscoders, attribution graphs, grokking, reasoning across the page, distillation, reinforcement, and evaluation. The Desk Volume carries the derivations, protocols, and a Bench of sixty-nine laboratory exercises.
